# Day 01: What AI Really Is (and Isn't)

**Part 1: Foundations** · Target runtime: 12 min (range 10 to 15 min) · 4K UHD · Cream background · Voice-over by host

## Prompt

Create a complete 12 min educational YouTube explainer video in 4K (3840 x 2160, 16:9, 30 fps) for Day 1 of a 15-day beginner course called "AI Fundamentals in 15 Days". The lecture title is "What AI Really Is (and Isn't)". Use a warm cream paper background throughout, flat 2D explainer motion graphics, and clean browser-window frames for references to public documentation web pages. Do not generate any narration or voice; the host will record the voice-over separately, so leave room for it and keep on-screen text short. Follow the visual style, scene list and references below exactly.

### Learning goals

- Define AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI, and show how they nest
- Separate real capabilities from hype
- Preview the 15-day roadmap

### Visual style (apply to every scene)

- **Resolution and format:** 3840 x 2160 (4K UHD), 16:9, 30 fps, H.264 or ProRes master. Title-safe margins of 5% on all sides.
- **Background:** warm cream paper, solid `#F7F1E3`, with a very subtle paper grain and a soft vignette. Never pure white, never dark mode.
- **Ink and text:** charcoal `#2B2B2B` for text and outlines; secondary text `#6B6458`; soft shadows `#E6DCC6`.
- **Part accent color:** Teal `#2A9D8F` for highlights, active elements and the progress bar. Supporting accents: mustard `#E9C46A`, muted red `#C8553D` (only for errors, myths and warnings).
- **Typography:** rounded geometric sans (e.g. Poppins or Inter) for headings, 96 to 140 px; body labels 48 to 64 px. Code in a monospace font (e.g. JetBrains Mono) at 44 px or larger on a rounded off-white code card `#FFFBF2` with a thin charcoal border.
- **Illustration style:** flat 2D explainer graphics, thick rounded outlines (6 to 8 px at 4K), soft drop shadows, simple icons, no photorealism, no stock footage.
- **Motion:** smooth ease-in-out, 0.4 to 0.8 s per element, gentle overshoot on pop-ins, elements build in one idea at a time. Camera moves are slow pushes and pans only. No flashing, no fast cuts.
- **Mascot:** "Token", a small round friendly robot drawn in the same flat style, appears on the journey map and occasionally reacts in scenes.
- **Web page references:** show each public doc page inside a clean rounded browser-window frame (no real browser chrome, just three dots and the URL in a pill), floating on the cream background with a soft shadow. Zoom and highlight the relevant section with an accent-colored rounded rectangle and an arrow callout. Show the URL clearly at the bottom of the frame for 3 seconds or more.
- **Logos:** use plain text labels for company and model names instead of official logos unless brand assets are licensed.

### Audio

- **No AI narration.** The host records the voice-over separately. Leave the voice-over track empty.
- Light, warm background music bed at about -28 LUFS, ducking under voice. Soft UI sound effects (pops, whooshes, clicks) at low volume.
- Keep each scene's visuals readable without audio, and hold the final state of each beat 1 to 2 seconds so it can be stretched to match the voice-over.

### Scene-by-scene storyboard

Timings are targets; stretch or trim each scene to fit the recorded voice-over.

**Scene 1 · 0:00 to 0:30 · Cold open hook**

- Visuals: Fast montage of everyday AI drawn as flat icons on the cream canvas: a phone unlocking with a face, a spam folder catching an email, a map rerouting, a chat window typing. Each icon pops in with a soft bounce, then all collapse into a single glowing dot.
- On-screen text: "You used AI 20 times before breakfast."
- Voice-over cue (host): Hook: AI is already everywhere. Ask: but what actually is it?

**Scene 2 · 0:30 to 0:50 · Course journey map**

- Visuals: Introduce the recurring 15-stop journey map: a winding dotted path across the canvas with 15 numbered circles grouped in four colored parts. A small round robot mascot ("Token") walks to stop 1, which pulses.
- On-screen text: Day 1 of 15: What AI Really Is
- Voice-over cue (host): Welcome to the series; what we'll build by Day 15.

**Scene 3 · 0:50 to 3:00 · Beat 1: The nesting circles**

- Visuals: Four concentric circles draw in one at a time, outer to inner: Artificial Intelligence > Machine Learning > Deep Learning > Generative AI. Each ring gets a label and two example icons placed inside it (chess engine, spam filter, face recognition, ChatGPT). Camera slowly pushes in to the innermost ring.
- On-screen text: AI > ML > Deep Learning > Generative AI
- Voice-over cue (host): Define each layer in one sentence with one everyday example.

**Scene 4 · 3:00 to 5:15 · Beat 2: 70-year timeline**

- Visuals: Horizontal timeline scrolls left to right. Milestones pop up as cards with year, icon and one line: 1956 Dartmouth workshop, 1997 Deep Blue, 2012 ImageNet/AlexNet, 2017 'Attention Is All You Need', 2022 ChatGPT, today (open-source and frontier models). A progress bar under the timeline fills as the camera pans.
- On-screen text: 1956 · 1997 · 2012 · 2017 · 2022 · Today
- Voice-over cue (host): Tell the story quickly; emphasize 2017 transformer and 2022 ChatGPT.

**Scene 5 · 5:15 to 7:15 · Beat 3: Narrow vs general, predictive vs generative**

- Visuals: 2x2 grid builds in. X axis: Predictive to Generative. Y axis: Narrow to General. Example icons slide into quadrants. A dashed outline marks 'General AI (AGI)' as not here yet.
- On-screen text: Predictive vs Generative · Narrow vs General
- Voice-over cue (host): Where today's tools sit, and what AGI would mean.

**Scene 6 · 7:15 to 9:15 · Beat 4: Myth vs fact**

- Visuals: Three split-screen cards flip like playing cards. Front: myth in a muted red label. Back: fact in the part color. 1) 'AI thinks like a human' / 'It finds patterns in data.' 2) 'AI is always right' / 'It can be confidently wrong (hallucinations).' 3) 'AI will replace every job' / 'It changes tasks; people who use it well gain the most.'
- On-screen text: Myth / Fact
- Voice-over cue (host): Bust each myth with one concrete example.

**Scene 7 · 9:15 to 11:15 · Hands-on demo**

- Visuals: Browser-frame mockup on the cream canvas showing the Teachable Machine homepage and a chatbot page. Animated callout arrows and highlight boxes point to buttons as the viewer is guided. Leave the browser area as a clean placeholder if real screen recording will be inserted.
- On-screen text: Try it: 3 AI tools in 10 minutes
- Voice-over cue (host): Walk through the homework task.

**Scene 8 · 11:15 to 11:45 · Recap**

- Visuals: Three-bullet summary card slides up; each bullet ticks with a check mark.
- On-screen text: 1. AI is a family of techniques  2. Generative AI is the newest layer  3. Powerful, but not magic
- Voice-over cue (host): Recap in three sentences.

**Scene 9 · 11:45 to 12:00 · Teaser**

- Visuals: Mascot walks from stop 1 to stop 2 on the journey map; stop 2 glows.
- On-screen text: Tomorrow: How Machines Learn
- Voice-over cue (host): Preview Day 2.
