# Day 02: How Machines Learn: ML in Plain English

**Part 1: Foundations** · Target runtime: 12 min (range 10 to 15 min) · 4K UHD · Cream background · Voice-over by host

## Prompt

Create a complete 12 min educational YouTube explainer video in 4K (3840 x 2160, 16:9, 30 fps) for Day 2 of a 15-day beginner course called "AI Fundamentals in 15 Days". The lecture title is "How Machines Learn: ML in Plain English". Use a warm cream paper background throughout, flat 2D explainer motion graphics, and clean browser-window frames for references to public documentation web pages. Do not generate any narration or voice; the host will record the voice-over separately, so leave room for it and keep on-screen text short. Follow the visual style, scene list and references below exactly.

### Learning goals

- Explain learning from data instead of hand-written rules
- Tell supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning apart
- Understand training vs testing, overfitting and data quality

### Visual style (apply to every scene)

- **Resolution and format:** 3840 x 2160 (4K UHD), 16:9, 30 fps, H.264 or ProRes master. Title-safe margins of 5% on all sides.
- **Background:** warm cream paper, solid `#F7F1E3`, with a very subtle paper grain and a soft vignette. Never pure white, never dark mode.
- **Ink and text:** charcoal `#2B2B2B` for text and outlines; secondary text `#6B6458`; soft shadows `#E6DCC6`.
- **Part accent color:** Teal `#2A9D8F` for highlights, active elements and the progress bar. Supporting accents: mustard `#E9C46A`, muted red `#C8553D` (only for errors, myths and warnings).
- **Typography:** rounded geometric sans (e.g. Poppins or Inter) for headings, 96 to 140 px; body labels 48 to 64 px. Code in a monospace font (e.g. JetBrains Mono) at 44 px or larger on a rounded off-white code card `#FFFBF2` with a thin charcoal border.
- **Illustration style:** flat 2D explainer graphics, thick rounded outlines (6 to 8 px at 4K), soft drop shadows, simple icons, no photorealism, no stock footage.
- **Motion:** smooth ease-in-out, 0.4 to 0.8 s per element, gentle overshoot on pop-ins, elements build in one idea at a time. Camera moves are slow pushes and pans only. No flashing, no fast cuts.
- **Mascot:** "Token", a small round friendly robot drawn in the same flat style, appears on the journey map and occasionally reacts in scenes.
- **Web page references:** show each public doc page inside a clean rounded browser-window frame (no real browser chrome, just three dots and the URL in a pill), floating on the cream background with a soft shadow. Zoom and highlight the relevant section with an accent-colored rounded rectangle and an arrow callout. Show the URL clearly at the bottom of the frame for 3 seconds or more.
- **Logos:** use plain text labels for company and model names instead of official logos unless brand assets are licensed.

### Audio

- **No AI narration.** The host records the voice-over separately. Leave the voice-over track empty.
- Light, warm background music bed at about -28 LUFS, ducking under voice. Soft UI sound effects (pops, whooshes, clicks) at low volume.
- Keep each scene's visuals readable without audio, and hold the final state of each beat 1 to 2 seconds so it can be stretched to match the voice-over.

### Scene-by-scene storyboard

Timings are targets; stretch or trim each scene to fit the recorded voice-over.

**Scene 1 · 0:00 to 0:30 · Cold open hook**

- Visuals: A cartoon programmer frantically writes if-statements to detect spam; the list grows off-screen. Freeze frame, then a model icon calmly draws a boundary between spam and non-spam dots.
- On-screen text: "What if the computer wrote the rules?"
- Voice-over cue (host): Hook: rules break, data teaches.

**Scene 2 · 0:30 to 0:45 · Journey map**

- Visuals: Mascot at stop 2.
- On-screen text: Day 2 of 15: How Machines Learn
- Voice-over cue (host): Where we are.

**Scene 3 · 0:45 to 3:00 · Beat 1: Rules vs data**

- Visuals: Split screen. Left: 'Traditional programming' Rules + Data -> Answers. Right: 'Machine learning' Data + Answers -> Rules. Arrows animate and the boxes swap positions to show the flip.
- On-screen text: Programming: Rules + Data -> Answers  |  ML: Data + Answers -> Rules
- Voice-over cue (host): The core idea of ML in one picture.

**Scene 4 · 3:00 to 5:00 · Beat 2: Features, labels, model**

- Visuals: A house icon breaks into feature tags (size, rooms, location) flowing into a model box; a price tag pops out. Then a scatter plot appears and a regression line fits itself to the points step by step, wobbling less each step.
- On-screen text: Features -> Model -> Prediction
- Voice-over cue (host): House price example; training vs inference.

**Scene 5 · 5:00 to 7:15 · Beat 3: Three ways to learn**

- Visuals: Three doors on the canvas open one at a time. Door 1 Supervised: labeled cards sorted into cat/dog bins. Door 2 Unsupervised: scattered dots self-group into colored clusters. Door 3 Reinforcement: a small robot tries paths in a maze and collects stars, with a reward counter.
- On-screen text: Supervised · Unsupervised · Reinforcement
- Voice-over cue (host): One example each; where LLMs use each type.

**Scene 6 · 7:15 to 8:45 · Beat 4: Train/test split and overfitting**

- Visuals: A deck of data cards splits 80/20 into Train and Test piles. Then a curve wiggles through every training point (overfit, labeled red), then a too-straight line (underfit), then a smooth good-fit curve (green).
- On-screen text: Overfit · Underfit · Just right
- Voice-over cue (host): Why we hold back test data.

**Scene 7 · 8:45 to 9:30 · Beat 5: Garbage in, garbage out**

- Visuals: A funnel: messy, biased data pours in, a skewed prediction comes out. Then clean, balanced data pours in and a fair prediction comes out.
- On-screen text: Data quality = model quality
- Voice-over cue (host): Bias in data, briefly.

**Scene 8 · 9:30 to 11:30 · Hands-on demo**

- Visuals: Browser-frame mockup of Teachable Machine 'Image Project'. Animated callouts: add class 1, add class 2, record samples, Train, Preview. Placeholder area for real screen recording.
- On-screen text: Train your first model
- Voice-over cue (host): Walk through the homework.

**Scene 9 · 11:30 to 11:50 · Recap**

- Visuals: Three-bullet card with checks.
- On-screen text: 1. ML learns rules from data  2. Three learning types  3. Test on unseen data
- Voice-over cue (host): Recap.

**Scene 10 · 11:50 to 12:00 · Teaser**
